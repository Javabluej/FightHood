## Local implementation status

The seven existing reference fighters have repaired local timelines. Kenny and Pratham remain additional local characters. Mbapu's local damage now follows the observed normal/boss distinction. Sutti Babu, Rail Raja, the Budget system and the seasonal portal are recorded as reference additions; they are **not implemented or shown as playable** in this project. Their models, complete behavior and animation timing have not been established.

This is a content refresh and independently authored repair, not a claim of exact animation parity. Local timing, hit data and movement constants remain authored values.
